Prototype 001 · First concept

Silkie 001

A fictional dinosaur-inspired interpretation of the Silkie chicken. This concept keeps the breed’s recognizable fluffy appearance while imagining a generalized theropod-inspired snout.

Concept overview

Base breed

Silkie (kura jedwabista), selected for its distinctive soft plumage, crest and feathered legs.

Visual inspiration

Generalized theropod anatomy, interpreted as a fictional snout with small teeth on the upper and lower jaws.

Features retained

  • Fluffy Silkie-like plumage
  • Characteristic crest
  • Feathered legs
  • Familiar overall chicken silhouette
  • No additional tail modification

Fictional profile

The concept imagines an omnivorous animal with a greater proportion of animal-based food. This is a creative design note, not real husbandry advice.

Important scientific note

Silkie 001 is a fictional speculative design for art and science communication. It is not a real genetic-engineering project, does not describe a viable biological modification, and no real animals are being modified.
